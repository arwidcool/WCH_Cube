### What the bug actually was
Not what the report assumed. The muxes were never greyed, disabled or covered —
every option was present and clickable throughout. Three separate things made
choosing HSE *look* like it did nothing:

1. **No RCC coupling** (engine) — `S.periph.RCC.settings['High Speed Clock (HSE)']`
   stayed `Disable`, PA1/PA2 were never claimed, the chip never repainted. Fixed.
2. **Identical numbers** — CH32V006's default crystal is 24 MHz, exactly the HSI
   rate, so SYSCLK read 24 before and after. Nothing on screen moved.
3. **`.cnode.off` is opacity .45** — a non-feeding branch is drawn exactly like a
   disabled control. Still open (→AGENT-3).
