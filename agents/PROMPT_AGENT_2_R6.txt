P0 - DELIVERABLE D: THE CLOCK SCHEMA HOLDS A SECOND PLL AND A PER-PERIPHERAL SOURCE MUX.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Today clock.pll is ONE object and clock.prescalers has ONE source per tap. CH32H417 has
  five PLLs (RM 3.3.4) and eight peripheral clock muxes (RM 3.4.13, RCC_CFGR2); they are
  absent by declaration, sysclk.sources lists 3 of the 7 SYSPLL_SEL allows, and the app
  computes NOTHING for the USB 48 MHz, LTDC pixel or ETH clocks.
    1. clock.plls: a map of named PLLs - inputs:, multipliers:, optional dividers:, an output
       name other taps can cite. clock.pll stays as the alias for the SYS PLL: five parts do
       not change.
    2. A tap whose source: is a LIST is a mux: the clock tab draws a <select>, clockCalc()
       computes from the chosen one, the .wchproj round-trips it, undo/redo covers it.
    3. sysclk.sources may name a secondary PLL's output.
    4. FORMAT.md gets the block; validate_mcu.py gets the checks (REQUEST to AGENT-1 with the
       shape); tests/clock_ui.test.js's five-part sweep and planted breaks cover the new
       controls (REQUEST to AGENT-3).
  ACCEPTANCE IS ONE NUMBER ON THE CLOCK TAB: USBFS 48 MHz computed from USBHS_PLL / 10, matching
  RM 3.4.13, on CH32H417, in a real browser.
